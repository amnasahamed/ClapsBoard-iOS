Frequently Asked Questions

How do I use the Camera Overlay (PiP)?

Tap the Camera icon in the top toolbar to activate your front camera. You can drag the camera view anywhere on the canvas to position yourself while you teach.

Can I import my own documents?

Yes! Go to the Docs tab and tap "Import Document". ClapsBoard supports PDF and PowerPoint files.

How do I schedule a class?

Navigate to the Plan tab and tap the + button to add a new class session to your calendar.

Is the canvas really infinite?

Yes, you can pan and zoom freely in any direction. Just use two fingers to pan and pinch to zoom.
