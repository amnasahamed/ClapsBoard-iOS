Privacy Policy

Effective Date: December 11, 2025

Last Updated: December 11, 2025

🔒 ClapsBoard is designed with privacy in mind. We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to ClapsBoard servers.

Overview

ClapsBoard is a digital whiteboard and creativity app developed by Claps Learn. This Privacy Policy explains how the app handles information when you use our application.

Information We Do NOT Collect

ClapsBoard is an offline-first application that prioritizes your privacy:

  • No Account Required – The app works without any login or registration
  • No Personal Data Collection – We do not collect names, emails, or any personal identifiers
  • No Server Transmission – Your drawings, documents, and calendar entries stay on your device. Voice Channel audio is sent only peer-to-peer to participants you connect with.
  • No Analytics or Tracking – We do not use cookies, analytics, or tracking technologies
  • No Third-Party Data Sharing – Your data is never shared with third parties

Device Permissions

ClapsBoard requests the following permissions solely for app functionality. All data accessed through these permissions remains on your device:

📷 Camera Optional Used for the picture-in-picture camera widget for tutorials or presentations. Camera feed is displayed locally and never recorded or transmitted.
🎙️ Microphone Optional Used for Voice Channel during live teaching. Audio is sent peer-to-peer over the local network only to connected participants and is not recorded or sent to ClapsBoard servers.
📡 Local Network Optional Used to discover and connect nearby Voice Channel participants. No local network data is sent to ClapsBoard servers.
🌐 Internet Required Used only for loading external links you may choose to open (such as the ClapsLearn website). No app data is transmitted.

Local Data Storage

All content you create in ClapsBoard is stored locally on your device:

  • Whiteboard drawings – Stored in device memory during your session
  • Exported images – Saved to your device's storage when you choose to export
  • App preferences – Basic settings are stored in on-device app storage

You have full control over this data and can clear it at any time through your device settings.

Children's Privacy

ClapsBoard is safe for users of all ages. Since we do not collect any personal information, there are no special concerns regarding children's data. The app can be used freely in educational settings.

Third-Party Services

ClapsBoard does not integrate with any third-party services that collect user data. The only external connection is the optional link to the ClapsLearn website, which is governed by its own privacy policy.

Data Security

Since all data remains on your device and is never transmitted to our servers, your information is as secure as your device itself. We recommend:

  • Keeping your device password-protected
  • Regularly updating your device's operating system
  • Being mindful of who has physical access to your device

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
